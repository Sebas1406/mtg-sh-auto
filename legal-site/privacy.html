Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MTG Auto Publisher handles information when generating creator content and interacting with connected publishing platforms such as TikTok.

1. Information We May Process

Depending on the feature being used, the service may process:

  • basic account and profile information returned by platform integrations;
  • access tokens and technical authorization data required for API access;
  • images, captions, hashtags, and post metadata selected by the creator;
  • technical logs needed for debugging, auditing, or failed-post recovery.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to:

  • authenticate the creator with connected platforms;
  • prepare and publish creator-approved content;
  • store operational logs and improve reliability of the publishing flow;
  • comply with legal requirements and platform policies.

3. Content Ownership and Control

The creator remains responsible for the content they generate, upload, review, and publish. The service does not claim ownership of creator content.

4. Sharing of Information

Information may be shared only with service providers or connected platforms when necessary to complete authentication, storage, delivery, or publication. We do not sell personal information.

5. Data Retention

Data is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for publishing, troubleshooting, recordkeeping, or compliance purposes.

6. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect stored credentials, media assets, and post metadata. However, no storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your Choices

You may stop using the service at any time and may revoke platform access through the relevant third-party account settings where supported.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact the app operator through the same website or account channel where the tool is provided.
